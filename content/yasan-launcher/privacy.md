This privacy policy applies to the Yasan Launcher app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Yasan Ghaffarian (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Freemium service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

## Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include information such as

- Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address)
- The pages of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages
- The time spent on the Application
- The operating system you use on your mobile device

Depending on the features you use, the Application may also collect or process information such as app opens, app launches, launcher interactions, apps installed or visible on your device, launcher configuration, app organization, spaces or context features, personalization data, session duration, device information, operating system version, crash data, language, country or region, and unique device identifiers.

The Service Provider does not sell your personal information and does not show third-party ads in the Application.

The Application may use location information when you enable location-based launcher features. This can include background location when you grant the Android background location permission, so that location-based launcher behavior can work when the Application is not visible. You can revoke location permissions through your device settings.

If you grant notification access, the Application may read notification information, including notification content, while the permission is enabled. This access is used locally for launcher notification features, such as displaying notification-related state or letting you act on notifications from the launcher. You can revoke notification access through your device settings.

When you use search features in the Application, your query may be sent to the selected external provider so that provider can open or return the requested result. Product analytics, when enabled, does not require the exact query text and may use general metadata such as the result type, selected provider, or query length.

The Service Provider may use information you provide voluntarily, such as when contacting support, to respond to you or provide important notices related to the Application.

## Opt-Out Rights

Analytics and diagnostic reporting are off by default. You can opt in during onboarding or later in the Application's Privacy screen, which is available from the main settings screen. Choosing "Accept All" during onboarding enables both analytics and diagnostic reporting. You can change these choices later in the Privacy screen, which also shows the tracking identifiers used by the Application.

You can also limit certain data collection by revoking Android permissions granted to the Application through your device settings, although some features may stop working after a permission is revoked.
